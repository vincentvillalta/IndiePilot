## Discretionary decisions
- **UUID library:** `uuid@^11` with `v7` export (time-ordered v7), generated
  app-side via `$defaultFn(uuidv7)`. `pg_uuidv7` Postgres extension was NOT
  pursued — adds operational dep, app-side generation is simpler and Neon
  Postgres extensions add cold-start friction.
- **Audit log columns:** `ip` is `text`, not `inet`. Drizzle 0.45 doesn't
  ship a first-class `inet` builder; `text` is round-trip safe and we can
  ALTER to `inet` later without data loss.
